Rooms

Stick a coloured paper on each door matching the colour here. Patients who cannot read the room name can still match the colour on the screen.

Test rooms (X-ray, lab, ...)

Each test room runs its own queue with its own letter, e.g. X1, X2 for X-ray. Numbers can be given at reception before seeing the doctor, or from the doctor's phone when a test is asked for. Pick a letter that is different from the urgent and booked letters above.

Numbering

Many clinics use 1, some use 101.
Urgent patients get E1, E2, ... so they never clash with walk-in numbers.
Booked patients get A1, A2, ... a third separate series.

Who gets called first

Urgent patients come before everyone in all three options.

Behaviour
